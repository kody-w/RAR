# Night Watch

Your Brainstem looks after its own computer. Each chat it checks (at most every 10 minutes) free disk space, whether it had to start as plain grail, whether its helper services answer, and last night's drills. A new problem is told once, in one sentence. Say "check my computer" or "fix it" (safe fixes only: restarting its own services; it never deletes anything).

Unconfigured services and missing or invalid drill evidence are **unknown / not configured**, not passed. Drill reports require valid, nonzero result counts and a file modification time within the past 36 hours; stale or future-dated reports are unknown. Configured services need a live health response. Missing boot evidence is also unknown.

Spend Guard counts premium replies in this computer's journal only. All usage figures and warnings are **local estimates**, not billed usage, remaining account allowance, or a billing API reading; the owner-set limit never blocks requests.
