Distros

Every distro is a sealed pack: your own Brainstem for one job, on the unchanged grail. Rip one open to see every card inside. The last card is the summon card, with its QR code and chant (or say the chant at s.html). Add several to your cart and rip them all together: keep or drop each card, and everything you keep installs into your one Brainstem, all or nothing, in the same order whatever order you picked. Starter packs fill the cart for you.
