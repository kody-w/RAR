# Meeting Analyzer (@cat-agent-skills/meeting_analyzer)

Analyzes meeting content pasted as text or provided as audio/video transcripts. Delivers a structured intelligence report: explicit decisions and action items, participant persona profiles, and — most importantly — hidden insights: unspoken tensions, implicit risks, unresolved topics, and signals that were not made explicit during the meeting but are critical to the context.
