# RAR census, 2026-11-10

A count of what is in this repo, taken at commit `8b2474d`, to decide what stays. Nothing here
has been moved. Each "would move" line is a proposal for Kody to rule on, one at a time.

## The headline

- 9,239 tracked files serve **273 catalog entries** (226 agents, 45 stacks, 2 swarms).
- 63 files and 24 folders sit at the top level. 23 of the files are separate web pages.
- 8 of 226 agents are tier `verified`. 97 are `frontier`, 73 `community`, 46 `official`, 2 `private`.
- 42 of 45 stacks are `current`, 3 `retired`, **0 `staging`**. The proven-winner rule says a new
  thing starts in staging and moves up only when it has won.
- 14 agent categories. `general` holds 43 agents. Four categories hold 2 or fewer
  (`memory` 2, `b2b_sales` 1, `meta` 1, `energy` 1).
