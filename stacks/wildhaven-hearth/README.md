# Wildhaven Hearth

Your own AI, at home on your computer, that looks after itself. By Wildhaven Homes.

- **Talk to it; it never drops the conversation.** Adding or removing anything, even an update, happens while it keeps
  answering. Nothing you are in the middle of is cut off.
- **Add what you need, take out what you don't.** Every pack installs and uninstalls cleanly; nothing else breaks.
- **It looks after its computer, keeps private topics at home, and knows who inherits it.**
- **Built on the Brainstem, unchanged.** Hearth pins the Brainstem's own engine exactly and adds everything around it.

Install: `curl -fsSL https://kody-w.github.io/grail-layers/add.sh | bash -s -- wildhaven-hearth`
Or ask any AI that can run commands (GitHub Copilot in Agent mode by default) with the RAR Easy Mode skill:
"Give me wildhaven-hearth using RAR Easy Mode and test it for me".
