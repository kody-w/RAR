Stacks

A stack is a drop-in pack — agent(s), an optional rapp/1 §9 .egg, and a sha256-pinned pack.json — that plants something bigger than a single agent: a neighborhood, a fleet/lifecycle kit, or a private rapp-workspace/1.1 vault. Every file below is independently fetchable and sha256-verifiable; you can take a whole stack or cherry-pick files across stacks. Machine-readable index: api/v1/stacks.json.
